# About us

**REmatch** is an initiative led by developers, professors, and students of the [Department of Computer Science of the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile](https://dcc.ing.uc.cl/) and the [Millenium Institute Foundational Research on Data](https://imfd.cl/en/); however, the initiative is open to the entire community. Our purpose is to develop a new generation of regex engines and information extraction tools based on research in logic, automata theory, and algorithms (see [here](https://rematch.cl/research) for further references in this area).

The library is open source under the MIT License, and the code is available [here](https://github.com/REmatchChile). If you want to contribute to the project or have any further questions, contact us at [contact@rematch.cl](contact@rematch.cl).
